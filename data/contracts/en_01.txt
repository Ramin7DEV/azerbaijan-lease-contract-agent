OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT
Baku, 20 September 2026
Caspian Towers LLC (TIN 1700111222, the "Landlord") and Brightline Consulting LLC (TIN 1800333444, the "Tenant") agree as follows:
1. PREMISES
1.1. The Landlord leases to the Tenant office premises of 95 sq.m on the 12th floor of the business centre at 28 Nizami Street, Sabail district, Baku, for office use.
1.2. The Premises are handed over under an acceptance certificate signed by both parties.
2. TERM
2.1. This Agreement is concluded for 2 years from 1 November 2026 to 31 October 2028.
3. RENT
3.1. Monthly rent is AZN 2,500 including VAT, payable in advance by the 1st day of each month.
3.2. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to five months' rent. The deposit bears no interest.
3.3. The Landlord may increase the rent at any time at its sole discretion.
4. OBLIGATIONS
4.1. The Tenant shall pay for utilities and building service charges.
4.2. The Landlord shall not be liable for any defects of the Premises, including defects known to the Landlord at signing and not disclosed to the Tenant.
4.3. The Tenant may not sublet the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.
5. TERMINATION
5.1. The Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving the Tenant 5 days' notice.
5.2. If the Tenant terminates this Agreement early, the Tenant shall pay the rent for the entire remaining term as a penalty.
5.3. This Agreement terminates automatically upon sale of the Premises.
6. DISPUTES
6.1. Disputes shall be resolved by the courts of the Republic of Azerbaijan.
